# The Ideas library — every idea, in full

3 September 2026. Build 1W. **89 ideas**, grouped exactly the way the
Ideas screen groups them.

Rewrite any line here and I will put it in the app. You do not have to explain the
change. Add new ideas in the same shape and I will add them.

---

## How the screen filters them

Two filter rows sit at the top of the Ideas screen.

**Filter by time** — how far ahead you need to start:

- Today
- This weekend
- A week or two
- Needs a month

**Filter by who it helps** — the categories below. This is Jessica's own axis:
ideas are sorted by *who is helped*, never by what you do.

- Neighbors — 10 ideas
- Teachers & schools — 9 ideas
- Community helpers — 8 ideas
- Elders — 10 ideas
- Feed people — 11 ideas
- Animals — 6 ideas
- Mother Earth — 5 ideas
- Friends & family — 14 ideas
- Causes & voice — 16 ideas

Each idea also carries a **size** — `An hour`, `A weekend`, or `A village` —
which is shown but not filtered on.

Below every idea is one line the app calls the fact that removes the excuse.
It is the sentence that makes the idea doable rather than admirable.

# Neighbors

*10 ideas*

### Bake something for new neighbors

`Today · An hour`

Wrap it, add a handwritten tag. Don't overthink the allergies, the welcome is the point.

### Fresh smoke-alarm batteries for an older neighbor

`Today · An hour`

Bring the stepladder too. Test each one before you leave.

### Leave a review for a small business

`Today · An hour`

Free, takes four minutes, and it is worth real money to somebody local.

### Mow, rake, shovel, or clip back the overgrowth

`Today · An hour`

Without being asked is the whole act. Their strip of yard while you are doing yours.

### Share garden surplus door to door

`Today · An hour`

A bag on the porch with a note. Zucchini season exists for this.

### Take a neighbor's trash and recycling out, and back

`Today · An hour`

Collection day, both directions. Small enough to repeat every week without asking.

### Stock a Little Free Library that needs it

`This weekend · A weekend`

The directory at littlefreelibrary.org lists them. Find one in an underserved spot, not the fullest one nearby.

### A card for every door in a senior housing building

`A week or two · A weekend`

December. Call the office first, they will tell you how many doors and which entrance.

### A block gathering that introduces two strangers

`Needs a month · A village`

First Tuesday of August is National Night Out. Introduce two neighbors who have never met.

### Welcome packages for people arriving somewhere new

`Needs a month · A village`

Find someone on the inside who can tell you who they are. Recruit two friends.

# Teachers & schools

*9 ideas*

### Email the principal about one teacher, and cc them

`Today · An hour`

One specific paragraph with evidence. It goes in a personnel file, praise to the manager travels.

### Write to a teacher who mattered

`Today · An hour`

`This weekend · An hour`

### Encouragement notes for a teacher to hand out

`This weekend · An hour`

`This weekend · A weekend`

Buy the boring things on it. Split it with a friend and cover a few items each.

### Pay off a school's lunch balances

`A week or two · A weekend`

### Shoes and underwear for the school nurse

One lunchtime a term. Teachers want real jobs described by real people.

# Where these came from

The library was built from **the shape** of Jessica's year, not its contents.
Every idea here is generalised: her blood drive became "donate blood", and no name
of hers, and none of her actual acts, travels into anybody's copy of the app.

The nine categories are her two overlapping lists — from her act 0 and her answers
doc — combined down without dropping anything: youth and teachers merged, unsheltered
neighbours and feeding friends split into Neighbors and Feed people, LGBTQ and missions
and democracy gathered into Causes & voice, and Mother Earth kept exactly as she
wrote it.

She edited the whole library herself on 29 August 2026 and that edit is in the app in
full — American spellings throughout, the school-run carpool cut ("I don't love this
one"), tennis balls moved to Animals, the two meal acts merged into one with the
recipient left open, and her own additions: the elder coupon book, the Girl Scout
cookies, the encouragement notes, the gift cards kept in the car, the meal honoring a
late parent, the voter postcards.
