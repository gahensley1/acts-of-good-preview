# The ideas library

**This file is where the ideas live.** The app's copy is built from it, so this
is the only place any of them are written. Change a line here, run the builder,
and the app matches. Nothing else needs editing, and the two can no longer drift.

    cd C:\Users\tony\Documents\aog-push
    python build-ideas.py

Or just say what you want changed and I will do it.

---

## How to write one

Every idea is a heading and a few lines under it. Blank lines are fine.

    ## Bake something for new neighbors
    when: Today
    size: An hour
    cost: Under $20
    fact: Wrap it, and write what is in it on the tag.

**The heading it sits under is the category.** To move an idea from Elders to
Neighbors, move the block. Nothing else to change.

**when** is how far ahead you need to start. One of:
`Today` · `This weekend` · `A week or two` · `Needs a month`

**size** is one of: `An hour` · `A weekend` · `A village`

**cost** is one of: `Free` · `Under $20` · `$20 or more`. Leave it blank if you
genuinely do not know — but a blank reads the same as free to somebody with no
money, so it is better to guess than to leave it.

**fact** is the line under the idea in the app: the one that removes the excuse.
It is the thing that makes an idea doable rather than admirable. Every one of
these should be TRUE, and checkable.

**why** (optional) is the reason, in lower case, used where the app explains
itself. **tag** (optional) is a short three-beat line. **needs** (optional) is a
list of what to bring, one per line, each starting with a dash.
