Where the letter is

You asked where this lives. It is the first thing anybody sees — a full-screen sheet that comes up before the year does, with her signature at the bottom and one button out of it. Here it is, whole. The gold marks are the words that gave it away.

In April of 2026, I turned fifty and wanted to mark the year in a meaningful way. So, I took on a challenge: Fifty Acts of Good in Year 50. To hold myself accountable, I logged each act and shared it on social media, hoping others might see a simple gesture of kindness and think, “Oh, I could do that.”

I quickly realized that the acts themselves were only part of the story. They became a catalyst for spending time with the people I love, whether baking for new neighbors, volunteering alongside friends, or tackling projects with family. Along the way, I kept a journal filled with ideas, notes, expenses, and memories.

This is the framework of that journal, designed to help you track your own journey and create a record of the good you put into the world. No one is keeping score. Choose a number that feels right for your life and begin when you’re ready.

What good can you spread?

Jessica
Savannah, GA

One line above it — something like “This app started with one person’s year. Here she is, in her own words.” Nobody has to work it out, and nobody feels they caught something they weren’t meant to.

The line comes out. Does anything take its place?

Your ruling and your question“Just remove ‘no one is keeping score’ — but you are just giving them tools. How is it perceived as keeping score? This is a tracker.”

You are right, and that is exactly why the line hurts

It is a tracker. Nobody objected to being able to count — they objected to being told nobody is counting by the thing doing the counting. The sentence apologises for the app’s own job, and an apology is what makes the count feel like a judgement.

Sherri said it straight: “Don’t hand me a counter and then apologize for it.” Take the apology out and the counter is just a counter.

The other true thing you can lean on: nothing leaves the phone unless they post it. There is no account, no server, nobody else’s year to be measured against.
