S7 · The five ways the app writes a date

All five are real, taken out of the running build just now. Every one is the same day: Saturday, 21 March 2026.

The thing worth knowing before you pick. You are American and the app is being built for an American store — and the two places that already read American, month first, are the square and the journal page. The three day-first ones are the ones nobody chose; they are just what got written. So the honest question is not "make them match", it is which order the app speaks in.

Either way the journal keeps its handwritten gold date — that is the one place a date should look written by hand, and both seats said leave it.
