One picture, three shapes

The app makes one square picture and hands it to whatever you are posting. A feed photo takes it whole. A story or a reel does not — it blows the square up to fill a tall frame and eats about a tenth off each side. That is the t missing from “thirty-seven” and the outside of the balloon gone.

The fix is not a smaller drawing. It is a picture already the right shape, with the card sitting in it at full width. Nothing inside the card moves, ever — the square stays the master for the book, the journal and the screen.

And Instagram makes every picture in one post share the shape of the first, so the photographs riding along have to be built the same way, or Instagram crops those instead.
