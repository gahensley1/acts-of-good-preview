What happens to the ones you rule

Right now the app guesses a pairing by counting the words a day and an act have in common, and it refuses to offer anything below a confidence floor. Every day you rule gets its act written down against it instead, so it is named rather than guessed — and the floor stays where it is for everything else.

Nothing on this page touches the library. These are pairings between days the app already names and acts already in it.
