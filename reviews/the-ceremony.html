The easy one

Act 0 is not a gate in front of the app. It is the introduction to it — the whole pipeline run once, end to end, on the only act that needs no event, no photograph and nobody else’s diary. You declare it, you are proud of it, and one is already down.

Why it belongs last

Setup collects. The ceremony commits. Every field the three steps gathered — the number, the reason, the hand, your name — is exactly what act 0’s card is made of, so by the time you arrive there is something real to declare, with your own words already on it.

And it answers the question every setup wizard leaves hanging: what happens now? Instead of being dropped on an empty grid, you are walked through the thing you will do twenty-five more times — on the one occasion where getting it wrong costs nothing.

The thesis

It is a tutorial wearing a ceremony

The pipeline has five stations, and a first act normally has to supply all of them at once: something that happened, a date, people, a photo, a story, a number. That is a lot to ask of someone on day one, and it is why first acts do not get logged.

Act 0 supplies all of it for free. The thing that happened is the decision. The date is today. The story is already written. The card is the one setup just built. So the person walks the entire route — write, card, post, confirm, journal — carrying nothing. They arrive at act 1 already knowing where everything is.

and the evidence for “one already down” is not soft: a commitment that has produced an artefact resists abandonment far better than one that has not.

Ruled A — with a correction

The zero square, and where the sash goes

What I drew was wrong, and the build says why. I showed the zero as a gold outlined tile. But .tile.planned is already gold outline on cream — in this app’s existing vocabulary, gold outline means intended, not yet done. A gold-outlined 0 at the head of the grid would have read as the zeroth empty box: precisely the thing on the list of what would embarrass Jessica.

Fill is the property that decides it. Outline is a promise; fill is a fact. So act 0 takes the done treatment — coral fill, white numeral — and is distinguished from the twenty-five by position, sitting out at the head of the grid, never by being hollowed out.

The sash means newest, and it moves. Two permanent sashes would collapse the word into decoration; a sash frozen on act 0 would still be claiming in March to be the most recent thing you did.

The sash moving off the zero and onto the one is the argument stated without words: the declaration was real, and then it was overtaken by living. It is the only moment in the whole year where the sash’s motion is felt rather than noticed. Worth protecting.

And the square is not pre-drawn. Through the whole intro the grid starts at 1. After “Did it go up?”, she lands on the year screen and the zero arrives, into a gap that opens for it. Granted things are already there when you get there. Earned things appear because you did something.

and the pace card never says 0 of 25 — it reads Begun until act 1 lands, then becomes the count it always was.

Why these choices

What the evidence supports

The gesture must be performed, not watched

Ritualised gestures raised valuation and savouring; random gestures did not, and watching someone else’s ritual did not. Hence a finger-drawn numeral, and no animation to admire.

Vohs, Wang, Gino & Norton, Psychological Science, 2013

Written and kept beats held in the head

Commitments written down and retained resisted revision far better than those merely thought — even with no audience. You need an artefact. Act 0 produces three: a card, a post, a page.

Deutsch & Gerard, 1955

Landmarks beat raw dates

Commitments spike after temporal landmarks — a new month, a birthday — because the landmark closes the previous period. If act 0 sets the start date, offer Today / The 1st / My birthday.

Dai, Milkman & Riis, Management Science, 2014

A pledge can forgive and still bind

NaNoWriMo’s agreement pre-forgives bad writing while holding the number. Same move: act 0 holds the number and the date, and forgives everything about how the acts turn out.

NaNoWriMo, Month-Long Novelist Agreement

Checked and discarded: the “sign at the top makes people honest” finding was retracted by PNAS in 2021 after failed replications, so nothing here rests on it. And the much-cited Duolingo “pinky-swear” commitment screen could not be verified in any source — folklore.

Two facts and no absolution — offering absolution would imply a fault.

The voice rejects one thing in the brief, and it is worth hearing. Pride has to come from the object — the card in her hand, her own handwriting on it — never from a line of app copy. Any sentence that names the feeling (“a good start”, “one down”, “nicely done”) gets cut. The easy win is something she notices, not something she is told.

And one collision to rule before any of it is drafted. Working-rule §3 keeps “Act n of N” opening every caption — settled, never relitigated. An act 0 caption reading “Act 0 of 25” is a different sentence from the settled one. Does act 0’s post carry the act line at all?

Guardrails

Where the easy win turns sour

No way past

No Skip, no Later, no Maybe. The ruling is that this must be done; one escape hatch makes it optional, and an optional ceremony is a form with nicer type. It is the last step of an intro she has already chosen to walk.

Not a lesson

It teaches by being real, never by explaining. No coach marks, no “this is where your acts go”, no tour. If a screen has to narrate itself, it is a tutorial pretending to be a ceremony rather than the other way round.

Nothing fails

No validation, no greyed-out button, no red message. The instant something here can be wrong, the easy win stops being easy.

Posting optional

Writing it completes act 0; sending it is hers to choose. Requiring the post makes the app depend on an audience she may not have. Not yet must be a real answer with no consequence.

No reproach

The app records that it went out and never brings it up. No count of days since, no reminder anyone is waiting. Her numbers kept; the app’s judgements about them never built.

No funnel

It recruits helpers for her year, never users for the app. No app name in the message, no “start your own” line.
