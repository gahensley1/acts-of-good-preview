# Sixteen days that need an act

**Acts of Good — for Jessica**
17 September 2026

The app keeps a short calendar of days worth marking. When one of them is coming up within three
weeks, it takes over the top of the home screen: it names the day and says a line or two about why
that day matters.

For eleven of them, the app can also offer an act to go with it. **For these sixteen it names the
day and stops.** Somebody who taps one gets the day and nothing to do about it.

**What's needed is one act for each — in your words.** No ideas are listed here on purpose. The
library is yours, and a suggested pairing tends to become the answer whether it's right or not.

Any of these can also be left as they are. A day named with no act is an honest answer; a wrong act
is worse than none. Mark it "leave it" and it stays as it is.
