Worth knowing first. The You screen is not silent today. The three rows under “About you” already read Not set in grey. So the question is not whether the screen says anything — it is whether arriving should be greeted, and how hard.

No toast at all. The dot on the tab becomes the same dot on the exact rows it meant, and grey Not set becomes coral Add. The most literally intelligent of the three — it does not describe the gap, it points at it — and there is nothing to miss and nothing to dismiss.

To rule

  • A, B or C — or a pair. C combines with either: the greeting names the gaps, the rows show which.
  • The words. All three read “Your card still needs a name and a birthday.” It names the gap without naming her, and drops to “a name” alone when only one is missing. Say if you want it different before it lands.
  • A only: whether the toast carries an Add button, or just says it and goes.
