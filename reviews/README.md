# The review pages

Rendered design reviews from the 30–31 August sessions. Every option is drawn in
the app's own colours and type, inside phone-sized frames, so a choice can be
made by looking rather than by reading a description.

**Open the `.html` files in a browser.** They also live online at the links
below, which is how G rules on them from his phone. Same page either way; the
links stay put when a page is updated.

## For another chat picking this up

These are **settled arguments, not proposals**. What was ruled is marked on each
page. Before re-opening anything drawn here, read `../HANDOFF.md` — it records
what was decided and why, including several things G ruled out that look like
good ideas on paper.

The pages are self-contained. They need no network except for the two type
families they load from Google Fonts.
