The quiet send

On the posting page there is a way to send an act to one person instead of putting it on Instagram. It is the gold envelope. Two of the seven testers said it is the only route they would ever use — and it sits at the very bottom, after everything else.

What you already gave me: the words. “Send it as a personal note to someone.” That is settled and it is in both pictures below.
What I still need: whether the envelope also moves up.

Why we are talking about it at all

Eileen, 68 — the one who does the most good and would be lost first. She said she would never post any of this. She has never called what she does “acts of good” and would be mortified to see the phrase on her phone with her name on it.

Kayla, 19 — the one the app has never met. Same conclusion for the opposite reason.

Neither of them spoke to the other. Both landed on the private send, and both had to go looking for it. Priya called offering it at equal weight a real piece of moral design — but right now it is not at equal weight. It is a footnote.
