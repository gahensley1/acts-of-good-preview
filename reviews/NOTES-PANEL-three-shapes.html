If B ends the list — how do you get a circle back?

The brief forbids a toolbar. Return on an empty line ends the list. As written there is no way back — so B needs one of these before it can be ruled.

1The ghost circle
I’ll have stamps and addresses ready.
Buy stamps
 

A hollow circle appears in the margin of the line you’re on, and nowhere else. Tap it and the line joins the list.

works dictating
No chrome, no mode, and it obeys the rule as written — you still tap a circle. The circle is simply always there, just unlit until you’re on the line.
2The hold menu
Buy stamps
+Add to your people
✉Invite them to this act
◯Make it a checklist line
×Leave it as words

This menu is already in the brief. It gains one row.

costs nothing
Nothing new to design or explain. But a hold is a deep gesture for something she’ll do constantly — that menu was drawn for occasional decisions, not everyday typing.
3Type a dash

- buy stamps

The trick every notes app uses: a dash or a bracket at the start of a line turns it into a list item.

reject
G dictates everything. Speech never reliably produces a leading dash or bracket, so this is a door only a typist can open — in an app built around the opposite assumption.

And going the other way: backspace at the start of an empty circled line takes the circle off, which is what Apple Notes does and what the separate-field architecture already handles as Backspace-at-0.

My recommendation is B with 1.

The brief already specifies the mechanic that makes B work — Return on an empty line ends the list — it just never says what you land in afterwards. Answer: a plain line, no circle. That is one sentence added, not a redesign, and the separate-textarea architecture handles a circleless line the same way it handles a circled one.

The ghost circle is the way back because it is the same gesture as the rule already written. “You tap the circle” stays literally true — on a plain line the circle is simply unlit until you’re standing on it. Nothing is added to the screen, and nothing has to be learned.
