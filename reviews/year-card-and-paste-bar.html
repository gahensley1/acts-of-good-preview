Ruling 4

The header climbing into the clock

Found it in the file, so this one needs no photograph. The safe-area inset is being paid for twice, in two places that disagree about who owns it.

Now Scrolled down, in Safari
9:41●●● ◼

Your year

act 3 of 25
The page scrolls under a header that is 94% white and blurred, so the clock and the battery show through it.

body carries padding-top:env(safe-area-inset-top), and the header is position:sticky; top:0. Sticky pins to the top of the viewport, and under viewport-fit=cover with a translucent status bar the viewport starts at the physical top of the screen. So the moment you scroll, the header leaves the padding behind and parks itself under the clock. Installed to the Home Screen a media query adds the inset back to the header’s own padding — which is why at rest the gap looks too big, and why the title jumps up by the height of the inset on the first scroll.

the inset is paid twice at rest, and not at all once stuck

A The header owns the inset Only sane fix
9:41●●● ◼

Your year

act 3 of 25
The header keeps the same height at rest and stuck, so nothing jumps and nothing passes behind the clock.

Drop padding-top from body, and give the header padding-top:calc(14px + env(safe-area-inset-top)) always instead of only when installed. One rule owns the inset, at rest and stuck, in Safari and on the Home Screen. No jump, no overlap.
