The Finish

Right now, finishing your year tells you that you have plenty of room left. Here is what I'd like to put there instead — and three small repairs the test run found on the way. Nothing below is built. Rule by letter and number.

1 · the counting

Half acts are gone

ruled · remove them

You said: remove half acts, it will fix some holes. It does — it closes four of the eight things the test run found, and it removes the question I was about to make you answer. An act is an act. Twenty-five squares, twenty-five acts, and the number on screen is simply how many squares are coloured in.

fixed Four holes close on their own

The count can no longer overshoot. "27 of 25" was halves being added up as wholes. Nothing adds up as anything now.

The slash problem disappears. No struck numerals means no line to get wrong on 12 or 25 — the whole of 6B is moot.

Act 1 can't become "act 0.5" and collide with act 0.

The card and the caption stop disagreeing — one said "Act 2.5 of 25", the other said "act 3 of 25".

gone What comes out

The "Mark to 2.5, as half an act" button on an act's menu, and its undo. The struck numeral on the card. The small coral "2.5" above it. The diagonally-split square on the grid. The journal's version of all of it.

One consequence worth naming. Jessica's own year had a half act — her 2.5 — and the app will no longer be able to draw one. That treatment was measured off her card and locked. You're overturning your own ruling here, which is entirely yours to do; I just want it on the record rather than quietly dropped.

Any half already saved becomes a whole act, keeping its square. Say so if you'd rather it went the other way.

7 · not building this, just asking

And then what?

Does the app offer another year?

When it's over, it's over — the grid is full and every screen still works, but there is nothing further to do. Should there be a way to begin a second year, keeping the first one readable in the journal? I'm not building it either way until you say. It changes the shape of the whole app.

How to answer

One line does it — 1A 3F 4A 5A 6 all 7 later, or however you'd rather have it. 3 and 6B are already ruled — 3 only needs its words picked. If none of the wordings are right, tell me which is closest and what's wrong with it, and I'll redraw rather than guess.
