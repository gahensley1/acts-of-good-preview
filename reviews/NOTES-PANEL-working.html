The notes panel

Jessica’s postcard-party note, in the app instead of Apple Notes. Use it — it is the real mechanic, not a picture of one.

Postcard party · 8 October

  • Tap a circle to tick it. Ticked text goes quiet — it never strikes through.
  • Return makes the next line, and it inherits the circle.
  • Return on an empty circled line drops the circle and ends the list. Carry on writing in sentences.
  • Stand on any plain line and a faint circle appears in the margin. Tap it and the line joins a list again.
  • Backspace at the start of a circled line takes the circle off. At the start of a plain line it joins the line above.

The text column never moves. Apple Notes shifts a line right when it becomes a checklist item; this doesn’t. The gutter is always there, holding a circle, a faint circle, or nothing — so a line changing kind never makes the page jump under your thumb mid-sentence.

Every line is its own field, which is what keeps dictation from landing in the wrong place. Nothing here is in the build yet.
