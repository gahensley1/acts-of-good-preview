## Verbatim

> In honor of turning 50, I'm celebrating with a year-long campaign that brings
> together time with the people I love and doing some good. I'm so excited that
> it warranted making a logo!
>
> A bit of the backstory, because this idea has been quietly building for a
> while:
>
> 1. When my friend Beth Howells turned 50 a few years back, she hosted a
> campaign along the lines of "Fifty Walks with 50 Friends." I thought it was
> such a lovely idea and loved following along on her journey. I tucked the idea
> of a "Year Fifty" campaign away in my brain and let it walk around for a
> couple of years.
>
> 2. Then, this past December, my friend Kate Carrico (who is one of the most
> golden-hearted people on planet earth and the founder of blooms4good) sent me
> the dearest care package, which included an iron-on patch that says, "DO GOOD
> RECKLESSLY." These three words spoke so loudly to my heart. It felt like a
> mantra I wanted to embrace for the upcoming year. I decided I wanted my "Year
> Fifty" campaign to be tied to this hymn.
>
> 3. So many ideas began forming – connecting travel to seeing friends and what
> good we could do together. I had so many thoughts that required putting pen to
> paper on this effort. I opened this beautiful journal that my dear friend
> Elaine Zhang (the original do-gooder and founder of baked4good) gifted me,
> which I had been squirreling away to record 'something special.' It was still
> sealed in its pristine clear packaging. When I opened to the first page, the
> journal read, "NO ONE CAN STOP ME." It was such a gorgeous nudge from the
> universe: do what you can, now.
>
> So here's the plan: 50 acts of good over the next year, done alongside
> friends—near and far.
>
> Some will be simple, some more involved: donating blood, picking up trash,
> feeding friends, thanking community helpers, celebrating neighbors, funding
> special projects, or giving someone a much-needed night off. Nothing grand
> required—just small, intentional acts that add up.
>
> Like many of you, I've been feeling the weight of what's happening in the
> world and the limits of what I can do. This is my way of focusing on what I
> can do—show up, connect, and put a little more good out into the world.
>
> To keep myself accountable, I'll be documenting each act along the way. And my
> hope is that all of you will see these gestures and say, "oh, I can do that!"
> Fifty acts is roughly one a week, which feels just right.
>
> I'll be reaching out to many of you—and if this effort resonates, I'd love for
> you to join me!
>
> Here's to the good people who continue to inspire me, and to the good ripples
> that hopefully come from this year-long journey.

## What this changes

**The origin is three people, not one.** Beth Howells' Fifty Walks planted it;
Kate Carrico's "DO GOOD RECKLESSLY" patch gave it a mantra; Elaine Zhang's
journal, opening on "NO ONE CAN STOP ME", was the push. The app's declaration
currently offers one blank — *"It began with ______"*. Her own answer needed
three numbered paragraphs.

**The app's act 0 ends on a line she did not write.** Ours reads *"…and if
you're up to some good — invite me."* Her act 0 says only:

> "I'll be reaching out to many of you—and if this effort resonates, I'd love
> for you to join me!"

The invite-me-too half comes from **act 21**, where she recalls having asked it
— *"I also asked my people to invite ME if they were up to some good good."* So
she did say it, but not in this post. It may have been in the comments, or said
in person. **Worth a decision:** keep our version, which is the ripple ruling
made explicit, or match what she actually posted.

**"Fifty acts is roughly one a week, which feels just right."** She sets the
cadence herself, in the declaration. The app already works this out and says it
the same way.

**Two more things the app could use.** Her list of what the acts might be —
*"donating blood, picking up trash, feeding friends, thanking community helpers,
celebrating neighbors, funding special projects, or giving someone a
much-needed night off"* — is a ready-made description of the Ideas library. And
*"Nothing grand required—just small, intentional acts that add up"* is the best
one-line description of the whole project anyone has written.

**Confirmed: the card on act 0 is the year card**, with no act number. Exactly
what the app draws.
